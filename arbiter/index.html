The Arbiter's Tells

Red attacks can be guarded. Blue attacks cannot, so dodge-roll through them. Guarding lasts a short window, and each successful block extends it, so press guard just before the hit lands. Blocking his blade opens him briefly to a counter. He only takes full damage while he is OPEN.

Auto mechanics (T on keyboard, View on gamepad) guards every red attack, rolls through every blue one, slips cages, charges and tractor beams, flees the Cinder Star, casts the matching spell on Sentinels, throws Thunder into stagger windows, drinks Elixirs when low and attacks whenever he is open. You can still move and fight alongside it.

First phase

Crossbow volleys
His crossbow glows before each shot. Guard reds, roll blues. In the nine-shot volley he drops after the sixth set: Airstep in.
Sweeping lance
He fires into the floor and drags the beam toward you. Wait a beat before guarding.
Charged severance
He crouches as his blade glows, then it flashes. Guard on the flash. Strike him during his second charge to force an early slash and an opening.
Spiral lance
A fiery dash that ends in a burst. Guard through it, then guard the three reticle strikes. He returns to his old spot for a moment.
Prism cage
Three shards form a pyramid around you. Roll out as the red lines brighten, then guard his rush. A Thunder cast after the second cage staggers him.
Severing charge
He sprints at you inside a pale sphere. It cannot be guarded. If it catches you he steals your blade: survive the water orb, slashes, ground spikes and ringed volleys by dodging and drinking Elixirs.
Thrown blade
His sword reappears behind you and strikes three times. Roll away from the first two, then guard the third to leave him open.
Tractor beam
Armored, he hovers and drags a beam that drains you and steals items, your Ember Charm first. Glide high in circles. After the third beam, cast Thunder to stagger him.

Nocturne Ascendant

Orrery
Gravity lifts you. Guard two reds, roll two blues, then Airstep. During the fakeouts stay still: if he appears in the same spot it is a feint, if he appears off to the side it is real. Then watch the far horizon.
Ashen Sentinels
Match the core: Fire for red, Blizzard for blue, Thunder for gold. Or circle until they hit the wall.
Echoes
Clones dive from above. Guard them. When twelve dive at once, guard once.
Cinder Star
A sun drags you inward. Run outward and roll his dashes: 4 ahead, 1 behind, 3 ahead, 2 behind, 3 ahead. Falling in brings a crushing verdict.

Second phase

Armored flurry
He adds one slash per repetition, up to eight. Guard and counter until the armor breaks.
Blue severance
Charged slashes turn blue: roll on the flash. Sometimes he fakes the first flash.
Lingering feint
Feints from one spot, then the real attack from another.
